Estonia Company Registration: 0% Corporate Tax on Retained Profit

By Mardo Soo, Founder & CEO, Consulting24 (X24Consulting OÜ) · Updated 2026-09-23

Short answer: We register your Estonian private limited company (OÜ) for €2,500, including a contact person and a legal address in Estonia for the first year. An Estonian company pays 0% corporate tax on profit it keeps or reinvests. Tax is only due when you pay profit out.

Estonia doesn't tax company profit when it's earned. It taxes it when it leaves the company. Keep the money in the business, reinvest it or build a reserve, and the corporate tax bill stays at zero, year after year. You don't need to live in Estonia or travel there to set one up.

Why Estonia: 0% tax on the profit you keep

Most countries tax company profit every year, whether you take it out or not. Estonia works differently. Corporate income tax is only charged when profit is distributed. Profit that stays in the company, or is reinvested in the business, carries 0% corporate tax, with no time limit.

When you do pay out a dividend, the company pays 22% on the gross amount. That's calculated as 22/78 of the net dividend. The 2% "security tax" on company profit that was planned for 2026 was cancelled in 2025, so the 22% rate stands.

Example: €100,000 profitKeep or reinvest itPay it all out
Estonian corporate tax€0€22,000
Left for you or the business€100,000 in the company€78,000 dividend to you
When tax is dueNever, while it stays in the companyIn the month the dividend is paid

This suits companies that grow by reinvesting, build up a cash reserve, or hold investments and pay out only now and then. A few rules keep the system honest. Non-business expenses, gifts and hidden profit distributions are taxed just like a dividend, and fringe benefits for staff are taxed separately. The accounts still have to be kept properly, and an annual report is filed every year.

How registration works

  1. Call or WhatsApp us. We agree the company name, what it will do, and who owns it.
  2. Identity check. You send your passport and proof of address, as Estonian anti-money-laundering rules require.
  3. Sign the power of attorney. We send it ready to sign and tell you exactly how to sign it from your country.
  4. We register the OÜ. Once the documents are signed, the Business Register usually registers a new company within a few working days.
  5. Your company is live. You get the registry code and the company documents, and the contact person and legal address are in place for the first year.

Is an Estonian company right for you?

A good fit if you:

  • Reinvest profit into growth rather than paying it all out every year
  • Want a simple EU company that trades in euros with customers across Europe and beyond
  • Run an online or service business that doesn't depend on local premises
  • Want to hold investments or build a reserve inside the company

Think twice if you:

  • Plan to pay out all the profit every year. The 22% then applies anyway, so the 0% advantage mostly disappears.
  • Live in a country that taxes foreign companies owned by its residents. Many countries have controlled-foreign-company rules, or tax a company where it is really managed.
A word on your home country: The 0% applies to Estonian corporate tax. If you live elsewhere, your country may tax the company's profit or your share of it under its own rules. Check with a tax adviser where you live before you register. A local director and a real office in Estonia help show that the company is genuinely run from Estonia.

FAQ - Estonia company registration

Is corporate tax in Estonia really 0%?

Yes, on profit that stays in the company. An Estonian company pays no corporate income tax on profit it keeps or reinvests, however long it keeps it. Tax is due only when profit leaves the company. Dividends are taxed at 22% (22/78 of the net amount paid out), and non-business expenses, gifts and hidden profit distributions are taxed the same way.

Do I need to live in Estonia or travel there?

No. You sign one power of attorney and we register the company for you. Because the management board will live outside Estonia, the law requires a contact person and a legal address in Estonia. Both are included in the €2,500 package for the first year.

Do I need e-Residency?

Not to register the company. We file it under your power of attorney. After registration, reports and filings are signed digitally, so e-Residency or a local director makes day-to-day administration easier. Our local director option covers that.

How much share capital does an Estonian company need?

The legal minimum is one cent, but we register companies with €100 of share capital. It isn't a fee: it's paid into your own company's bank account and stays company money.

Can you open a bank account for the company?

Bank account opening consulting is included in the local director option (€4,000 for one year). Banks make their own decisions and are careful with companies whose owners live abroad. So we prepare the application with you and approach the banks and payment institutions that fit your business.

What is not included in the €2,500?

  • The €100 share capital
  • Bookkeeping and the annual report (every Estonian company files one each year)
  • A local director
  • A real office
  • Extra powers of attorney (€250 each)

Everything else you need to register the company and keep it legally reachable for the first year is included.
