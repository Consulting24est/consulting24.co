About Consulting24 & Mardo Soo

Founded by Mardo Soo, Founder & CEO · X24Consulting OÜ (reg. 16971898, Tallinn, Estonia)

Who we are

Consulting24 is a crypto-licensing consultancy operating as X24Consulting OÜ (Estonian Business Register code 16971898), based in Tallinn, Estonia. Since 2018 we have delivered 500+ crypto licenses for exchanges, brokers, wallets, payment processors and token issuers, with direct delivery in Estonia, Lithuania and Panama.

Mardo Soo, Founder & CEO

Mardo Soo founded Consulting24 and leads jurisdiction strategy for every client. Over eight years he has personally advised on and helped structure 200+ crypto, fintech and financial-services entities across 15+ jurisdictions, including Panama, Lithuania (CASP), Estonia (CASP), Canada MSB, Cyprus and beyond. Connect on LinkedIn.

How we deliver

  • Direct delivery (we incorporate and file): Panama (company + AML/CTF; note Panama has no dedicated crypto licence today), Estonia (MiCA CASP) and Lithuania (MiCA CASP).
  • Advise & coordinate (we guide you and coordinate vetted local partners): most other jurisdictions, from the EU to offshore.
  • Comparison only: UAE (Dubai VARA, Abu Dhabi ADGM) - we cover these for comparison against Panama but do not file the licence.
